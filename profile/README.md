# BareBoard

Free hardware education. We can't open their hardware, so we open our findings.

Live at [bareboard.org](https://www.bareboard.org) — board-level teardowns, diagnostics guides, and standard operating procedures for real consumer hardware, published free forever. The site's largest section covers power mobility devices: exploded views of power wheelchairs, fault-code translations, and owner repair guides.

BareBoard preserves datasheets, manuals, and documentation for future generations.
